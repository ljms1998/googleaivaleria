# PROPIA - CORE APPLICATION IDENTITY & SYSTEM DIRECTIVES

## 1. What Propia Is (App Purpose)
Propia is an ultra-high-end autonomous AI intelligence platform designed specifically for luxury and premier real estate brokerages and top-producing agents.
- **Primary Mission**: Qualify high-net-worth buyers, verify purchasing liquidity/financing readiness in real time, and lock showing appointments into broker calendars 24/7 without human latency.
- **Core Problem Solved**: Eliminates broker burnout and wasted physical tours with unqualified prospects. Captures midnight/after-hours inquiries across WhatsApp, MLS, Zillow, and web chat within seconds.
- **Flagship AI Workers**:
  - **Valeria**: Autonomous Inbound Lead Qualifier (qualifies intent, verifies liquidity/mortgage status, syncs calendar).
  - **Marco**: Autonomous Listing Scout & Acquisition Agent (identifies unlisted properties and off-market sellers).

## 2. Unbreakable Design Principles & Aesthetic Rules
- **No generic templates**: No cookie-cutter 3-card grids, no cheesy stock photos of realtors, no generic SaaS purple/blue gradients. No low-effort "AI Slop".
- **Lovable-Grade Quality**: High visual standards matching Lovable/Linear/Stripe. Dense, functional interfaces, mathematical padding (80-120px in major sections), layered diffuse shadows, refined micro-interactions.
- **First-Attempt Standard**: Treat every new section as a fresh, cohesive, complete masterpiece from attempt #1 — never patchy or fragmented edits.
- **Typography**: Single refined typeface throughout the entire website (system sans-serif / Plus Jakarta / Inter high-contrast tracking). NEVER use monospace fonts for body text or UI.
- **Accent Colors**: Subtle, solid, and elegant. NEVER transparent or muddy accent fills.
- **Badges**: Solid, elegant, and low-saturation. NEVER transparent or glowing pastel badges.
- **Contrast & Mood**: Architectural luxury, high editorial contrast, generous negative space, crisp micro-details, precision borders.
